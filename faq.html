MnaPlayer+ — Frequently asked questions

2026-10-12

What is MnaPlayer+?

MnaPlayer+ is a free, ad-free music and media player for Android (a Windows version also exists). It plays your own music and video files and helps you discover legal online content: Creative Commons music, internet radio, podcasts and YouTube through the official YouTube player.

Is MnaPlayer+ free?

Yes. MnaPlayer+ is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking.

Does MnaPlayer+ collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no own servers. Your library, playlists, collections and listening history stay on your device. Online features are optional; when they are off, the app makes no network requests.

Can I download music from YouTube with MnaPlayer+?

No. YouTube videos play only in the official YouTube player, as YouTube's terms require. MnaPlayer+ offers downloads only for content whose licence allows it: public-domain and Creative Commons music (for example from Jamendo and the Internet Archive) and podcast episodes.

Which online sources does MnaPlayer+ use?

YouTube (official API and player), Jamendo and Openverse (Creative Commons music), the Internet Archive (public-domain and licensed recordings), podcast feeds found via the iTunes Search API, and the radio-browser.info directory of internet radio stations. Each source can be turned off separately.

Does it play music offline?

Yes. Your own files always play offline, and downloadable Creative Commons tracks and podcast episodes can be saved for offline listening.

Which languages and countries does it support?

The app is available in 26 languages and picks your phone's language automatically. Popular lists, radio stations and quick searches follow your phone's region.

Does it support voice search?

Yes. The microphone button uses Android's own speech recognition; MnaPlayer+ itself does not request microphone permission or record audio.

Is MnaPlayer+ on Google Play?

Not yet — it is coming soon to Google Play.
