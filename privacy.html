MnaPlayer+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Applies to the MnaPlayer+ app (Android package mna.playerplus) and its Windows version.

Summary

  • MnaPlayer+ has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • Your music library, playlists, collections, listening history and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Online features are optional. When "Online" is turned off in the app, MnaPlayer+ makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

To play your local files the app reads audio and video files that Android's media store or your chosen folders expose, and stores an index (titles, artists, durations, artwork cache), your playlists and collections, resume positions, listening statistics and recent searches in its private app storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share something (for example a collection link or a share card) with an app you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Online features (optional)

When you enable online search, the text you search for — and nothing that identifies you — is sent directly from your device to the services you have enabled:

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API and the official YouTube embedded player). MnaPlayer+ uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Requests carry the app's package name and signing-certificate fingerprint so the API key works only for MnaPlayer+. Videos play only in the official YouTube player; MnaPlayer+ does not download YouTube content or play it in the background. API data is kept only in memory for the current session.
  • Internet Archive (archive.org) — search and playback of public-domain and Creative-Commons recordings.
  • Openverse and Jamendo — search and playback of Creative-Commons music.
  • Apple iTunes Search API and the podcasts' own RSS feeds — podcast discovery and episodes.
  • radio-browser.info — internet radio directory; radio streams come from the stations themselves.

These services receive your IP address and the request, as with any internet connection, and handle it under their own privacy policies. You can turn each source, or all online features, off at any time in Settings → Online.

Downloads

Downloads are offered only for content whose licence allows it (public domain, Creative Commons, podcasts). Files are saved to your device's Music folder together with their licence and attribution.

Voice search

The microphone button opens Android's own speech-recognition service. MnaPlayer+ does not request the microphone permission and does not record audio; it only receives the recognised text. Depending on your device settings, the speech service may process audio on the device or on Google's servers under Google's policies.

Permissions

  • Music and video access — to find and play your files.
  • Notifications and foreground service — media controls and background downloads.
  • Internet and network state — only for the optional online features and the "Wi-Fi only" download setting.

Children

MnaPlayer+ is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
